About

I taught myself to knit in 2018 when raising my twin boys slowed down. After seeing a beautiful cable knit toque in a store, I wanted to make one. I probably should have started with something beginner friendly. I ended up with some odd-looking toques. I've learned a lot over the years, but still make some questionable knitwear. I love knitting, and it's become a bit of an addiction, so I decided to sell my creations. I hope others will enjoy them as much as I enjoy making them.
